Need a hand? Email budgetherosupport@proton.me and say what you were doing and what happened. It helps to include your device and your iOS or macOS version. I'll reply as soon as I can.

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings (the gear on the Overview), then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If you share Pro through Family Sharing, it appears once you're in the same family group.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In BudgetHero, go to Settings › BudgetHero Pro › Manage subscription. You can also do it in your device's Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. If you cancel during the free month, you won't be charged. If Pro ends, nothing is hidden or deleted. You just can't add more than the free limits allow.

I bought Pro for life but I'm still subscribed

Buying Pro for life doesn't cancel an existing subscription. Go to Settings › BudgetHero Pro › Manage subscription and cancel it, so you aren't charged again.

How do I sync between my iPhone and Mac?

iCloud sync is part of BudgetHero Pro. Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices, make sure iCloud is turned on for BudgetHero, and open the app on each. Settings › iCloud shows whether it's up to date. A new device waits briefly for your budget to arrive. Syncing can take a minute or two.

How do I back up and restore my data?

Go to Settings › Backup. Save a backup creates a file you keep wherever you like. Restore from a backup replaces everything in the app with the contents of that file. Receipt photos aren't included in backups.

Why is my payday in the wrong place?

Check Settings › Payday for the weekend rule (Friday before, Monday after, or on the day). To move a single payday, for example for a bank holiday, go to the Income tab and tap Change next payday.

How do I start again?

Settings › Redo setup removes your pay details and keeps your bills, purchases and categories. Settings › Erase everything deletes all your data and starts setup from scratch. With iCloud sync on, that happens on all your devices.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. With Pro and iCloud sync on, it's also in your own private iCloud. BudgetHero has no servers and I can't see your budget. See the privacy policy.
