BudgetHero Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

In short: BudgetHero doesn't collect your data. There's no sign-up, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking. BudgetHero has no servers of its own. Your budget stays on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. We can't see any of it.

This policy covers the BudgetHero app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. “We” means the developer of BudgetHero.

What you put in the app

You enter your pay, bills, purchases, pockets, savings pots, loans (including the name of anyone you lend money to), notes, categories and receipt photos. All of it is stored on your device. None of it is sent to us.

iCloud sync (BudgetHero Pro)

If you have BudgetHero Pro and are signed in to iCloud, your budget syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud database, and your currency and weekend payday setting sync through iCloud key-value storage. Apple runs these services. Only your Apple Account can read this data. We can't. Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple handles iCloud data.

On the free version, nothing leaves your device.

Buying Pro

Purchases and subscriptions go through Apple's App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have Pro. Apple gives us sales totals (for example, how many people started a free week), which don't identify you.

Camera and photos

With Pro you can add a receipt photo to a purchase. BudgetHero asks to use the camera only when you take one. If you pick a photo from your library, the app only receives the photo you chose. Receipt photos are stored with the purchase, on your device and in your private iCloud if sync is on.

Notifications

Reminders and spending alerts are set up on your device, using your budget on that device. They aren't sent through any server.

Backups

When you save a backup, BudgetHero creates a file and you choose where to keep it (Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on). The file isn't encrypted and contains your budget, so keep it somewhere safe. Receipt photos aren't included.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and basic usage numbers. They don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device.
  • To remove your iCloud copy: on iPhone go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › BudgetHero. On a Mac go to System Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage. Then delete the data.
  • Delete any backup files you saved yourself.

We hold none of your data, so there's nothing for us to delete or send you.

Children

BudgetHero isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's data.

Changes to this policy

If the way BudgetHero handles your data changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
